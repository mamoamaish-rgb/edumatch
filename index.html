ABOUT EDUMATCH

Helping students make informed decisions.

EduMatch is a web-based college and scholarship recommendation system developed for Grade 12 students of Notre Dame of Parang, Inc.

The system helps students explore college programs, schools, admission information, and scholarship opportunities that may match their preferences.

EduMatch is intended as a decision-support tool. Recommendations do not guarantee admission, scholarship approval, or acceptance by any institution.
